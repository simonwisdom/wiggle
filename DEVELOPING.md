# Developing Wiggle

(Was Morning Stretch.) **Name and look, 12 Nov 2026:** the name came from namegen ("the loosest, silliest little movement there is"). The look is cobalt blue (#2f5bea, #7c9bff in dark mode) on a pale blue-white, with a warm orange for the out-breath and Fredoka 600 for headings (OFL, `public/fonts/`). The logo is a white stick figure bouncing on cobalt, redrawn by hand as `public/icon.svg` from the picked codex-images sketch (`~/skywell/wiggle-brand/`).

A phone web app for the "Floor to standing" morning movement: pick what it's for (the morning, before a workout or after one), 5, 10 or 15 minutes and the body areas, and it builds the session, then plays it with a moving figure and a breath bar.

- **39 moves, a different session each day.** No equipment, tagged by area (hips, hamstrings, inner thighs, hip flexors, ankles, upper back, shoulders, spine) and by position. Each day it picks moves you haven't done lately, spreads them across areas, fits them to 5, 10 or 15 minutes and orders them from the floor up. Cat–cow opens and Stand still closes every morning session.
- **Morning, Warm-up or Cool-down.** Morning is the floor-to-standing session. Warm-up, for before a workout, uses moving stretches only on a quicker breath, opens with Cat–cow and ends with the Shake. Cool-down, for after one, is mostly long holds on a slow breath, runs from standing down to the floor, and leaves out the demanding moves.
- **Qigong.** A fourth kind of session: the Eight Brocades (Ba Duan Jin) in their fixed order (hold up the sky, draw the bow, separate heaven and earth, wise owl looks back, sway the head and shake the tail, hands down the legs to the feet, punch with angry eyes, bounce on the heels), on a slow breath, opened and closed by Stand still. 5, 10 or 15 minutes changes only the breaths per piece; body areas, gear, swaps and more/less don't apply, so the home screen hides them. Its pieces stay out of the other sessions (Morning keeps the combined "Hold up the sky, draw the bow").
- **Follows your focus.** In the morning, pick the body areas that feel tight. For a warm-up or cool-down, pick the muscle groups you are training, named as a gym app names them (Chest, Back, Shoulders, Arms, Core, Glutes, Quads, Hamstrings, Inner thighs, Calves): each maps to the body areas that prepare or release it and to the moves that work it most directly. Whole body, Upper body or Lower body above the map picks a whole half in one tap (tap a picked half again for the whole body). Most of the session works your picks, with more reps. Each kind of session remembers its own picks.
- **Warm-up, then cool-down.** Open Wiggle within three hours of a warm-up and it is already set to Cool-down for the same muscle groups.
- **Your gear**: Foam roller, Stick or Massage gun, off until picked, and remembered until you untick it. Picked gear adds its moves (7 roller, 5 stick, 6 massage gun) for the muscles you chose, about one gear move for every three minutes so it tops up a session rather than taking it over. Massage gun moves are timed (10-second strokes, each side), not on the breath. The gear is drawn on the figure in orange: the roller on the floor under the body, the stick in the hands, across the shoulders or along the back, the gun in the hand nearer the spot.
- **Keep my music playing** (home screen, off by default): Wiggle's sound mixes with music from another app instead of pausing it. The phone mutes this kind of sound when it locks or is on silent, so in this mode the session pauses when the screen goes off.
- **More or fewer moves.** Under today's session, Fewer moves and More moves change the count by one; Wiggle still picks which moves, by building the session for a little more or less time until it has that many, so the length follows. It resets when the length or the kind of session changes. Not for Qigong.
- **Swap** any move, on the home screen or while it plays, for another in the same position taking about the same time.
- **More, Less or Never** for each move changes how often it comes up.
- **Learns from how it felt.** After each session: Easy, About right or Hard. The last three answers move the next session: easy adds reps to the harder moves (Beast to crab, World's greatest stretch); hard slows the breath and shortens them.
- **A figure with a bit of character.** The figure has shaded blue limbs, orange joints and a soft shadow. Under Your figure on the home screen, you can give it a head: plain, smiley, beanie, sweatband, sunglasses, cat, sun or robot. Its eyes close on the out-breath and blink now and then. Only our own characters; no famous faces.
- **Screen and voice together.** Every session runs with a sound guide as well as the figure, unless you turn the voice off in the player (it stays off until you turn it on again): a voice names each move and how to do it, a wave of soft sound with a quiet hum in it swells on the in-breath and ebbs on the out-breath, as one continuous sound, and a chime marks each new move, so it carries on when the phone is out of sight, or locked. Pause, play and skip work from the headphones and the lock screen. It pauses other audio on the phone.
- Keeps the screen awake, chimes at each new move, works offline once loaded, and can be added to the home screen. History stays in the phone's browser.

## Run it

```sh
npm run dev      # http://127.0.0.1:8731
npm test         # the session builder's tests
npm run deploy   # Cloudflare Workers, static assets only
```

## Files

- `public/poses.js`: the hand-made stick-figure poses, from `~/skywell/projects/morning-movement/video/assets/poses.js` (the video and the A4 card use the same file); it now also shares `joints`. The start on hands and knees still uses them, and the whole figure falls back to them if `clips.js` doesn't load.
- `public/clips.js`: real motion for every move, made by `~/skywell/projects/stick-motion-test/tools/build-app-clips.js` (don't edit by hand). Every move comes from a short demo video through MediaPipe (sources in each `data/yt_<move>.spec.json` there), except the massage gun moves for glutes, upper traps and chest: no whole-body video of them was found, so `tools/hand-gun-clips.py` there places one arm by hand on a standing body traced from the Hold-up-the-sky clip. No motion comes from HY-Motion: its licence excludes the UK, so its cat–cow, beast to crab, down dog to fold and those three gun moves were replaced on 17 Nov 2026. Qigong pieces 3 to 8 all come from one front-on Eight Brocades demo (Peter Chen); the punch is drawn side-on because the fist comes straight at the camera. A two-sided qigong piece has one "middle" moment between its two sides, so each breath plays a short stretch of the clip, forward or backward (a test keeps it under 3.5× the demo's speed). Each clip lists the moments where the plan's key poses happen.
- `public/figure.js`: how the figure looks: the shaded limbs and the heads. It draws from the same 44 numbers as a clip frame, so clips and hand-made poses look alike. Colours come from the `--fig-*` tokens in `app.css`, so dark mode follows. The gear (`gear.js`) draws in layers behind and in front of it.
- `public/clipplay.js`: plays a clip between two key moments, stretched to the breath, and blends where a move changes clip or meets a hand-made pose. Moves done on both sides swap which limbs are inked, fading over a breath.
- `public/plan.js`: the moves, and how a session is built from a length, a focus, recent answers and what was done lately. Library moves are a type (flow, hold, alt, loop), and their segments come from the type; their poses are named `move.key` (`move.key.F` for the other side) and play from clip `yt_move`.
- `public/figure3d.js`: the 3D figure. Pre-rendered clips in `public/clips3d/` (made by `tools/figure3d`, see its README; publish with `node tools/figure3d/clips.mjs --publish`) play in step with the session's timeline: two stacked videos swapped at the shared pose, a smooth clock steered toward the sound track, speed set by the breath. Moves with no 3D poses (the Qigong pieces, the gear moves) show the flat figure, fading over 0.3 s. The flat head you picked is drawn on top, placed from each clip's head track. Switch Flat / 3D in the figure sheet, or in the player; the choice is saved and Flat is the default. Gear is drawn only on the flat figure (every gear move is flat anyway). The service worker stores the clips whole, only once you pick 3D, answers range requests from the stored file, and keeps them across app updates (bump `CLIPS` in `public/sw.js` when they are rendered again).
- `public/app.js`: home, player and done screens.
- `public/gear.js`: draws the gear on the figure from the body's points in each frame (clips track only the body).
- `public/sound.js`: the sound guide. A locked phone stops a web app's timers but keeps playing a track, so the whole session is mixed into one WAV on the phone at Start (voice clips, breath tones, chimes) and that track is the player's clock. The voice is Kokoro `am_michael`, recorded by `tools/build-voice.sh` into `public/sound/voice.pack` (with its index, `voice.json`): run it again after changing a caption, a move's name or its "how", or a test fails for the missing line.

## Ship

It runs at https://wiggle.simonwisdom.workers.dev (Cloudflare Workers, static assets, free). Change it in a worktree on a branch, then run `bin/ship` from there after Simon says ship: it tests, pushes `main` to github.com/simonwisdom/wiggle (public, so commits use the GitHub no-reply address set in this repo's git config) and deploys. On the phone it is a home-screen app that works offline; a new version arrives the next time it opens with signal.
